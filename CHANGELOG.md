# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-09

- First version: Job Center NPC and Job Portal (E, `/jobs`, ph-tablet tile).
- Teams of up to 4 with create / join / leave / kick and a team pay bonus (100 / 115 / 130 / 150 %).
- Delivery (box van, shop back doors) and Recycling (collection truck, bins) jobs.
- Work vehicle spawned at the Job Center parking lot, fuelled, keys to the leader, $250 deposit refunded on return.
- GPS road route to every stop, on-screen tracker, carry animations and props.
- One bank payment per member at the end; cancelling early pays the stops already done.
- `/jobcenter ped` and `/jobcenter parking` (admin) move the Job Center in game.
